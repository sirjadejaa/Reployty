# Staff Quick Guide — Reployty Loyalty Terminal

> **For**: Cashiers and front-desk staff
> **Time to read**: 3 minutes

---

## 1. How to Sign In

1. Open the Reployty link on your phone or register tablet
2. Enter your **email address** and **password** (provided by your manager)
3. Tap **Sign In**
4. You will see your business dashboard

> **Trouble signing in?** Contact your manager or the support number posted near the register.

---

## 2. How to Find a Customer

1. Tap **Customers** in the menu
2. Use the search bar to find a customer by **name** or **phone number**
3. Tap on their name to see their loyalty card, visit history, and stamps

---

## 3. How to Award a Loyalty Stamp

When a customer makes a qualifying purchase:

1. Go to the customer's profile
2. Tap **Award Stamp** (or use the stamp-awarding interface)
3. Confirm the stamp
4. The customer's digital card updates immediately

> **Important**: Only award stamps for qualifying purchases. Do not award multiple stamps for the same transaction.

---

## 4. How to Verify a Reward Voucher

When a customer says they have a reward to use:

1. Go to **Rewards** → **Staff Terminal** tab
2. Ask the customer for their **voucher code** (shown on their phone, e.g., `BREW-8492`)
3. Type the code into the terminal
4. Tap **[Verify Code]**

**You will see one of these results:**

| Result | What it Means | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **Green check — VALID** | The reward is real and has not been used | Proceed to redeem (Step 5) |
| 🔴 **Red alert — ALREADY REDEEMED** | This voucher was already used | Politely inform the customer it was already redeemed |
| 🔴 **Red alert — EXPIRED** | The voucher validity period has passed | Inform the customer the voucher has expired |
| 🔴 **Red alert — INVALID** | The code does not match any voucher | Ask the customer to double-check the code on their phone |

---

## 5. How to Redeem a Voucher

After verifying a voucher shows ✅ VALID:

1. Tap **[Confirm Redemption Now]**
2. The status changes to **REDEEMED** with your name and timestamp
3. Provide the customer with their reward (e.g., free coffee)

> **The voucher is now permanently marked as used.** It cannot be used again by anyone.

---

## 6. What to Do When a Voucher Is Invalid

- **Already Redeemed**: Tell the customer: *"This reward was already used on [date]. Would you like to continue collecting stamps toward your next reward?"*
- **Expired**: Tell the customer: *"This voucher expired. Keep collecting stamps — you're close to earning another reward!"*
- **Code Not Found**: Ask the customer to show you the code on their phone screen. If it still doesn't work, contact your manager.

---

## 7. What to Do When the Network Is Unavailable

If Reployty is not loading or showing an error:

1. **Check your internet connection** (Wi-Fi or mobile data)
2. **Refresh the page** (pull down or tap the refresh button)
3. **Wait 30 seconds** and try again
4. If it's still not working, **tell the customer**: *"Our digital system is temporarily unavailable. I can note your purchase and we'll add your stamp once it's back online."*
5. **Write down** the customer's phone number and what they purchased
6. Contact your manager or the support number

> **Do not** try to manually give a reward without verifying it in the system.

---

## 8. What to Do If a Customer Has Trouble Joining

If a customer scans the QR code but has issues:

1. Make sure they are pointing their **phone camera** at the QR standee (not a QR scanning app)
2. The Reployty page should open automatically in their browser
3. They enter their **phone number** and receive a **6-digit verification code** via text message
4. If they don't receive the code, ask them to wait 60 seconds and try again
5. If it still doesn't work, write down their phone number and tell them: *"We'll help you sign up next time you visit!"*

---
