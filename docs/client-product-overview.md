# Reployty — Product Overview for Business Owners

## 1. What is Reployty?

**Reployty** is a customer loyalty and retention platform built specifically for local, brick-and-mortar businesses—like cafés, restaurants, bakeries, salons, and specialty shops.

> **Our Core Goal**: *Turn first-time visitors into loyal regulars.*

Most local businesses see dozens of new faces every week, but up to 70% never come back a second time. Paper punch cards get lost or forgotten in wallets, and asking customers to download a heavy mobile app creates too much friction at the checkout counter.

Reployty replaces paper cards with a **fast, browser-based digital loyalty pass** that lives right on the customer's phone—no app download required.

## 2. How the Customer Experience Works

```text
1. SCAN                     2. JOIN                     3. COLLECT & REDEEM
Customer scans the          Customer enters phone       Digital card updates instantly.
counter standee QR code     number & verifies with      Earns stamps with each visit
with their phone camera.    a fast 6-digit text code.   and unlocks free perks.
```

### 1. No App Download Required
Customers do not need to visit the App Store or Google Play. Scanning your countertop QR code immediately opens your business's branded page in their mobile browser.

### 2. Instant Phone Verification
The customer enters their mobile number and confirms with a fast one-time passcode. Their account is created in seconds, and they can save the digital pass to their phone's home screen.

### 3. Digital Stamp Card & Rewards Progress
Every visit, your counter staff awards a stamp (or points). Customers see a visual digital card that updates in real time, showing exactly how close they are to their next reward (for example: *"3 more stamps until a free signature coffee"*).

### 4. Simple, Fraud-Proof Voucher Redemption
When a customer earns a reward, Reployty issues an 8-character voucher code (e.g. `BREW-8492`). Your cashier enters or scans this code on the staff terminal. The system checks it instantly, marks it as redeemed, and prevents duplicate use.

## 3. What Business Owners Can Manage

Reployty gives you a clean, simple dashboard accessible from any phone, tablet, or laptop:

* **Customer Directory & Visit History**: See your customers by name, phone number, visit count, last visit date, and favorite branch.
* **Loyalty Programs & Rewards**: Set your own rules. Choose how many stamps are needed to earn a perk, set reward expiration dates, and update your reward menu anytime.
* **Cashier Terminal**: A fast, dedicated web terminal for front-desk staff and cashiers to look up vouchers and confirm redemptions in seconds.
* **Customer Groups & Inactivity Alerts**: Automatically identify your top VIPs, new guests, and customers who haven't visited in 30+ days.
* **Automated Win-Back Messages**: Set up automatic reminder messages (via SMS or WhatsApp) to invite inactive regulars back with a special perk.
* **Multi-Branch Support**: If you operate more than one store or location, you can manage all branches from one master account while keeping staff and visits organized by location.

## 5. What a 30-Day Pilot Involves

If you choose to test Reployty with a pilot, here is what we do together:

1. **Setup (1–2 Days)**: We configure your store's branding (logo, colors), define your loyalty reward (e.g., *Buy 5, Get 1 Free*), and set up your counter staff.
2. **Launch at the Counter**: We provide a clean counter standee with your custom QR code. Staff simply invite customers to scan while paying.
3. **Weekly Check-ins**: We check how many guests joined, review how smoothly checkouts ran, and adjust reward rules based on customer feedback.
4. **Zero Lock-In**: At the end of the pilot, you decide whether to continue. You always own your customer list and can export your data anytime.

## 6. What Requires Additional Configuration

To ensure complete transparency, here is what is ready today versus what requires specific setup before a live launch:

* **Ready in our Core System**: Digital stamp cards, QR counter joining, cashier redemption terminal, customer CRM directory, automated retention workflows, and multi-branch management.
* **Configured During Onboarding**: Setting up your custom domain (e.g., `rewards.yourbusiness.com`), connecting your approved SMS or WhatsApp Business messaging sender, and connecting your payment gateway for monthly subscription billing.
