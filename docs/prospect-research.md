# Reployty Prospect Research & Market Qualification Guide

## 1. Purpose & Guiding Principles

This guide helps the developer systematically identify, research, and qualify local brick-and-mortar businesses for a first pilot without relying on expensive lead databases, web scrapers, or spam automation.

**Core Rules**:
* **Manual & Ethical**: Use only publicly available business contact information (Google Business profiles, public social pages, physical storefront visits).
* **Respect Privacy**: Never scrape personal phone numbers or harvest private directories.
* **Focus on Fit**: Qualify businesses where repeat customer frequency is natural and frequent.

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) for First Pilot

For the very first pilot, prioritize a business matching this profile:

### Ideal Characteristics
* **Type**: Independent single-location café, specialty coffee roastery, artisan bakery, or neighborhood barbershop.
* **Customer Traffic**: At least 30–80 daily transactions.
* **Current Loyalty Method**: Uses nothing, or uses paper punch cards that get lost.
* **Owner Presence**: The owner is on-site at least 2–3 days per week and actively cares about regular customers.
* **Checkout Setup**: Has a clean counter area where a small A6 standee can sit next to the cash register.
* **Internet**: Reliable Wi-Fi or cellular connectivity at the counter.

### Red Flags to Avoid for First Pilot
* High-volume franchises with strict corporate POS mandates (e.g. Starbucks, Subway).
* Businesses with zero repeat customer potential (e.g. airport kiosks, tourist gift shops).
* Owners who are never on-site and delegate everything to unmotivated, high-turnover temporary staff.
* Businesses with complex, slow checkouts or no counter area for a standee.

### Step 1: Physical or Digital Scouting
* Focus on a walkable commercial neighborhood with independent, high-energy coffee shops, bakeries, or salons.
* Search Google Maps or Instagram for keywords: *"Specialty coffee [Neighborhood]"*, *"Artisan bakery [City]"*, *"Barbershop [Area]"*.

### Step 2: Record Prospect Data
Log the business in your lead tracker with the following standard fields:
* **Business Name**: Full legal or trading name.
* **Category**: Café, Bakery, Salon, Restaurant, etc.
* **Location / Area**: Physical street address and neighborhood.
* **Public Channels**: Google Business link, Instagram handle, public business WhatsApp/Phone.
* **Owner / Manager Name**: If publicly available on their website ("About Us") or Instagram bio.
* **Current Loyalty Method**: Observed method (Paper stamp card, POS phone prompt, None).
* **Observed Pain Point**: E.g. *"Lots of morning commuters grabbing espresso; paper stamp cards stacked at counter."*

## 5. Prospect Qualification Checklist

Before initiating outreach, score the business on these 7 criteria:

| Criteria | Yes / No | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Natural Repeat Visits** | [ ] | Do customers return at least once or twice a month? |
| **2. Simple Counter Area** | [ ] | Is there space next to the register for a 4x6" acrylic standee? |
| **3. Owner is Accessible** | [ ] | Can you speak directly with the owner or general manager? |
| **4. Low POS Complexity** | [ ] | Can staff operate a simple mobile web terminal alongside their existing register? |
| **5. Willingness to Experiment** | [ ] | Does the owner seem entrepreneurial and open to new ideas? |
| **6. Staff Stability** | [ ] | Does the business have consistent, friendly counter staff? |
| **7. Clear Value Incentive** | [ ] | Can they easily offer a low-cost, high-perceived-value perk (e.g. free coffee, pastry, or haircut discount)? |

**Scoring**:
* **6–7 YES**: **Ideal Pilot Candidate** (Schedule in-person or direct introduction).
* **4–5 YES**: **Secondary Candidate** (Reach out with initial feedback inquiry).
* **<4 YES**: **Disqualify** (Do not spend outreach time).
