# Reployty Discovery Call & Objection Handling Playbook

## 1. Product Positioning & Elevator Pitch Variations

Use these natural, jargon-free explanations depending on the channel and context:

### A. The 10-Second Introduction (Casual / In-Store)
> *"Reployty replaces paper punch cards with a digital stamp card that customers open instantly on their phone from a counter QR code."*

### B. The 30-Second Explanation (Networking / Quick Call)
> *"We help independent local businesses—like cafés and salons—turn first-time customers into regular visitors. Instead of paper cards that get lost or apps people refuse to download, customers scan a small QR standee at your counter to earn stamps and unlock rewards right in their phone browser."*

### C. The 60-Second Product Pitch (Owner Meeting)
> *"Most local shops see dozens of new customers each week, but over half never return. Paper stamp cards don't tell you who your customers are, and mobile apps have too much friction. Reployty gives your store a fast, branded digital loyalty pass. A customer scans your counter QR code once with their camera, verifies their number in ten seconds, and collects digital stamps with each visit. Your staff can verify rewards in five seconds at checkout, and the system automatically reminds regulars if they haven't visited in a month."*

### D. The Discovery Call Opening
> *"Thanks for taking ten minutes to speak with me, [Name]. Before I show you anything, my main goal today is to learn how you currently handle customer repeat visits and what your checkout flow looks like. If Reployty sounds like a practical fit for [Business Name], great. If not, your feedback is still genuinely helpful."*

## 3. Discovery Call Question Framework

Before showing the product, ask these questions to diagnose their real operational reality:

1. **Repeat Customer Habits**:
   * *"Roughly what percentage of your daily customers are first-timers versus familiar regulars?"*
   * *"Do you currently have any way to bring back a regular who hasn't visited in a month or two?"*
2. **Current Loyalty Methods**:
   * *"Have you ever tried paper stamp cards, a punch card app, or POS loyalty?"*
   * *(If yes)*: *"What worked well, and what was frustrating about it?"*
   * *(If no)*: *"What kept you from setting one up in the past?"*
3. **Counter & Checkout Speed**:
   * *"During your busiest peak hours, how much time does a cashier spend with each customer?"*
   * *"Would having staff spend 3–5 seconds verifying a voucher on a phone or tablet slow down your register line?"*
4. **Perks & Rewards**:
   * *"What is a simple, high-margin perk you could offer that customers would genuinely get excited about? (e.g. Free pastry, drink upgrade, 10% off a future visit)"*
5. **Customer Comfort**:
   * *"Do your customers frequently scan QR codes for things like Wi-Fi or digital menus?"*
6. **Pilot Success Criteria**:
   * *"If we set this up for 30 days, what would convince you at the end of the month that it was worth keeping?"*

## 4. Factual Objection-Handling Reference

| Objection | Factual, Respectful Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"We already use paper stamp cards."** | *"Paper cards are great because they're familiar, but they have two big drawbacks: customers lose them (which kills the motivation to return), and you have no way to reach someone who hasn't visited in 6 weeks. Reployty gives the exact same simple stamp card feeling, but it stays saved on their phone and lets you send an automated reminder when someone drifts away."* |
| **"Our customers won't want to scan QR codes."** | *"That was very true a few years ago, but today almost everyone knows how to scan a menu or payment QR with their native phone camera. The big difference with Reployty is that scanning doesn't make them download a 100MB app—it opens immediately in their mobile browser in less than two seconds."* |
| **"My counter staff won't want to use another system."** | *"Staff simplicity is our top priority. Front-desk staff don't have to navigate complicated screens. They have a single-screen web terminal on their phone or register where they type an 8-character voucher code, see a green checkmark, and tap 'Redeem'. It takes about five seconds."* |
| **"We don't have time to set this up."** | *"We handle the entire setup for you. We configure your logo, colors, and reward rules, and we hand you a ready-to-use counter standee. You and your staff don't have to build anything."* |
| **"What happens if our store Wi-Fi goes down?"** | *"Because Reployty is a responsive web application, staff can run the cashier terminal using normal cellular data (4G/5G) on any phone. Customers can also access their digital pass using their own mobile data connection."* |
| **"How much does it cost?"** | *"We offer a completely free starter tier for up to 100 customers so you can test it risk-free. For growing stores with up to 1,000 customers and automated messaging, plans start at ₹1,499 per month. For our first pilot partners, the entire 30-day trial is 100% free with no contract."* |
| **"Where is customer data stored, and is it private?"** | *"All data is stored in our secure, dedicated database. We never sell customer contact information or share it with third parties. Your customer list belongs entirely to your business, and you can export it at any time."* |
| **"Can I cancel anytime?"** | *"Yes, absolutely. There are no annual lock-in contracts or cancellation penalties. If you ever decide to stop, your account simply deactivates and you take your customer data with you."* |
| **"Does it work on any phone or tablet?"** | *"Yes. Reployty is designed to work smoothly on iPhones, Android phones, iPads, and counter POS tablets via standard modern browsers (Safari, Chrome)."* |
| **"Can I use it across multiple locations?"** | *"Yes. You can manage multiple branches under one owner account, track which location each customer visits most, and let customers earn or redeem stamps across all your branches or only specific ones."* |
